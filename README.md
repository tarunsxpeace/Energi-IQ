# ⚡ EnergyIQ — AI-Powered Smart Campus Energy Management Platform

> **Cut campus electricity waste by up to 28% using real-time IoT telemetry, occupancy automation, and explainable Machine Learning.**

---

## 📌 Executive Summary

Modern college campuses suffer from significant energy waste due to HVAC and lighting left running in empty classrooms, unoptimized schedules, and undetected equipment anomalies. **EnergyIQ** bridges the gap between hardware sensors and facility management by combining real-time IoT monitoring, automated occupancy-driven control, ML energy forecasting, and explainable AI diagnostic alerts into a unified, responsive dashboard.

---

## ✨ Core Features

* 📊 **Live Telemetry & Campus Heatmap:** Real-time tracking of voltage, power draw (kW), and power factor broken down by building, floor, and room.
* 🤖 **Occupancy-Based Automation:** Sensor fusion algorithm (PIR motion + camera count + timetable schedules) that automatically triggers relays to shut off idle equipment.
* 📈 **ML Energy Forecasting:** Predictive time-series modeling (Prophet) that projects next-day and next-week energy demand based on historical trends, weather forecasts, and academic holidays.
* 🚨 **Anomaly & Ghost Load Detection:** Isolation Forest algorithm detecting abnormal power spikes, stuck HVAC compressors, and after-hours phantom loads.
* 💬 **Explainable AI Eco-Assistant:** Integrated LLM assistant providing plain-language diagnostic summaries, root-cause explanations, and actionable energy-saving recommendations.
* 🏆 **Sustainability Leaderboard & ROI Tracker:** Live tracking of total kWh saved, utility cost reductions, CO₂ footprint offsets, and inter-building green rankings.
